My journey began in the tactile world of Android development, crafting fluid interfaces and managing the intricate lifecycles of mobile applications. It was here I learned that beauty is secondary to performance—a lesson that fueled my transition into the deep waters of Backend Engineering.

Transitioning from Java on the client to Java on the server was a shift in scale, not just syntax. Today, I architect distributed systems where reliability and throughput are the primary metrics of success.

My philosophy is simple: Clean Code & Scalable Design. Whether it's a micro-interaction or a microservice, if it's not maintainable, it's not finished.
